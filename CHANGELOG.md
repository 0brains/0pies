# Changelog

Release notes for [0pi.es](https://0pi.es). Site releases are numbered by date
(`YYYY.MM.DD`); labs keep their own version on the home-page cards. The newest
release is first, and its number is the one in the site footer.

## 2026.10.02

### Regulatory Risk — laws checked 2 October 2026

The campaign's legal content was re-verified against primary sources (official
journals, treaty office records, statute texts, government gazettes). Cards
teach current law and say what changed where it differs from the AIGP Body of
Knowledge v2.1.

- **Council of Europe AI Convention (CETS 225):** taught as signed but not in
  force — only the EU has ratified (15 May 2026), 21 signatories. Four answer
  keys that said otherwise are corrected.
- **EU AI Act / Digital Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744):** national
  sandboxes due 2 August 2027 (was 2 August 2026); GPAI fines attributed to
  Art. 101; AI-literacy wording and Article 50 dates made consistent.
- **United States:** California SB 1000 (signed 30 Sep 2026) rewrite of the AI
  Transparency Act; Colorado's replacement law SB 26-189, now classed as
  sectoral; federal, New York and Illinois updates.
- **UK, Asia-Pacific and elsewhere:** UK machinery-of-government changes;
  Australia, Korea, China, Japan, Taiwan, India (now classed as sectoral),
  Peru's sector-by-sector compliance dates to 2029, Canada's bills C-34 and
  C-36, UAE and Kenya.
- **Timeline:** 43 milestones (11 new); items whose dates have passed now sit
  in "Already in force", so correct placements are no longer marked wrong.
- **"Just track the bill" cards** rewritten to reward preparing compliance
  ahead of a likely law, without implying a bill applies before it is enacted.
- Translations of corrected strings were withdrawn; those strings show in
  English until re-translated.

### Regulatory Risk — its own page

- New page at [0pi.es/risk/](https://0pi.es/risk/) that opens straight into
  the game; the same game still ships in the AIGP and Legislation labs.
- "Regulatory Risk" in the nav bar (also on mobile) and a featured banner on
  the home page with the date the laws were last checked.

  older game).

### News

- The news page is back. It was unavailable from 15 September to 2 October
  2026: it listed every story ever collected and outgrew the serving limit.
- The page now shows the newest 200 stories; every story stays on its month's
  archive page (`news.html?m=YYYY-MM`), linked from the Archive row. Share
  links open the story on its month page.

### Site

- Home-page counts corrected: 177 games, 4,014 scored cards (the Legislation
  card had drifted unnoticed).
- AIGP lab v1.1, Legislation & Regulatory lab v0.5.
