Risk and policy briefs

Open-source model governance remains contested in Washington. Reporting shows active debate inside the administration over whether open models need a more interventionist policy posture, especially where cyber capability and foreign access are concerned.
OpenAI is reportedly pressing for faster release reviews. That tension highlights the emerging trade-off between deployment speed and state-led evaluation of cyber-capable frontier systems.
Industry leaders are now openly discussing slowing frontier development. Public support from leading labs for international pacing mechanisms suggests a more serious tone around frontier risk than the market had a year ago.
Litigation and state-level consumer protection are widening. Beyond top-line federal policy, legal exposure is spreading through consumer protection, platform harms, and claims linked to safety, accuracy, and misuse.
