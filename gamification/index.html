How these work

▦Boards, not quizzes

Sort a whole bank of cards into place, then score the board. Question-and-answer practice lives in the exam app, not here.

↺Confident misses come back first

Progress is kept per item. The cards you were sure about and still got wrong resurface soonest, because those are the ones that fail you.

🧠Every reveal goes deeper

Hand any board to Claude, ChatGPT or Perplexity — with what you missed, or the whole concept when you got it right.

📜 The Terms 📜

There used to be a joke here saying there were no terms. There are now exactly two, and they are both good news.

One: it's yours. Every game, every card deck and every line of the build pipeline is public at github.com/0brains/0pies. Use it, study from it, fork it, rip it apart, wire it into your own study setup, run it offline on a laptop in a field. Schools, universities, charities, public research and government use are all explicitly fine.

Two: don't sell it. That's the whole restriction. No reselling it, no paid course or subscription built on top of it, no quietly bundling it into a commercial product. It's licensed under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 — the full text ships in the repo as LICENSE.

Being honest about the label: that makes this source-available rather than open source in the strict OSI sense, because the OSI definition doesn't permit a no-commercial-use rule. Want a commercial licence? Open an issue and ask.

Bits that aren't ours to give away. The AWS and Azure architecture icons stay under Amazon's and Microsoft's own terms (diagrams, training material and documentation only). Vendor names and logos are their trademarks. The world map is CC BY-SA 3.0 and share-alike, so it stays that way no matter what we say. It's all itemised in NOTICE.md — worth two minutes before you fork.

The boring-but-true bit. This is independent study material, not affiliated with or endorsed by the IAPP, Amazon, Microsoft or anyone else whose exam name appears here. We try hard to be accurate and we cite our sources, but regulation moves faster than anyone's content pipeline. Cards carry asOf dates for exactly that reason.
